# Computer-Science Figure Catalog

| Question | Preferred figure | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Where is the final-vs-candidate gap? | Stacked selection-gap accounting | final correct, available-but-wrong, miss, denominator |
| How does abstention trade coverage for error? | Risk/accuracy-coverage curve | threshold rows, coverage, answered error/accuracy |
| Does a method transfer across datasets? | Paired/faceted transfer plot | source and target results under comparable protocols |
| Which component matters? | Dot/bar ablation with uncertainty | matched runs, seeds or intervals |
| What decision is the method making? | TikZ decision-layer schematic | formal objective and visible inputs |
| How does evidence connect to candidates? | Evidence-candidate graph | explicit relation semantics and claim boundary |

For more than five series, facet instead of adding colors. Show seed variation
or intervals when available. Keep diagnostic, oracle, and deployable surfaces
visually separated rather than sorting all rows into one leaderboard.
